Questions

Does it cost anything for families?

No. The fair is always free for families.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes, kids are welcome.

Is there a directory?

Yes. The printed fair directory lists every exhibitor. Pre-order it online for $15 and pick it up at check-in, or buy it at the fair for $20.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. We choose venues with step-free access, accessible restrooms and nearby parking.

What if it snows?

There's a published snow date. Everyone who RSVPs gets an email if anything changes.
